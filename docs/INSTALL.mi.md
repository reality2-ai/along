# Tāutahia a Along, ka whakamahi tuimotu

> **He whakamāoritanga hukihuki nā AI.** Tērā pea he hapa kei roto. Kāore anō kia arotakengia e tētahi kaikōrero matatau ki te reo Māori. Ka puritia ngā ingoa tahua pūtirotiro ki te reo Ingarihi kia māmā ai te kimi.

> **He mahi akoranga — kei a koe te tūraru o te whakamahi.** I hangaia a Along hei mahi mō tētahi akoranga tuhi waehere me te āwhina AI. He taupānga tukutuku whakamātau mō te ako; ehara i te ratonga mana a Auckland Transport. Tērā pea he kōrero ngaro, he kōrero tawhito, he hapa rānei i ngā ara, ngā wā, ngā tohutohu hīkoi me ngā kōrero urunga. Tirohia tō haerenga me ō hiahia urunga ki AT i mua i te haere.

**Ka rere a Along i runga i tō pūrere. Ka tuku te pae tukutuku i te taupānga me ngā whakahou; kāore ia e whakahaere i ō rapunga haerenga. Ina oti te tikiake i te taupānga me ngā raraunga haere, ka mahi tonu ngā mahi matua ahakoa kāore he hononga, kāore rānei te pae e wātea ana.**

**Ka noho ō rapunga haerenga, ō ara kua tiakina, ō ritenga kua akohia me ō manakohanga ki tēnei pūtirotiro i tēnei pūrere.** Kāore e hiahiatia he pūkete, he ratonga tātari whakamahinga, he tuku hītori haerenga, he aroturuki papamuri rānei. Kāore a Along e tukutahi pūrere i tēnei wā.

## He kōrero wātaka, ehara i te aroturuki wā tūturu

**Ka whakamahi te taupānga Along tūmatanui i ngā wātaka kua tikiakehia. Kāore e whakaatu i ngā tauwāhi pahi, tereina, waka kōpiko rānei i te wā tūturu, i ngā takaroa, i ngā whakakorenga o nāianei rānei.** Ahakoa kei te tuihono, kāore ngā mahere haerenga me ngā papa wehenga e huri hei kōrero wā tūturu. He wā kua whakaritea ngā wā wehenga; he whakatau tata ngā wā hīkoi me ngā whakawhitinga. Kāore te tiaki haerenga e tāpui ratonga, e whakaū rānei i tētahi hononga.

Mō ngā wehenga me ngā tauwāhi waka o nāianei, whakamahia [AT Mobile](https://at.govt.nz/atmobile/), tirohia rānei [te paetukutuku AT i mua i te haere](https://at.govt.nz/bus-train-ferry/service-announcements/check-before-you-travel). Me whai hononga ipurangi ēnei hononga; ka whakatuwhera i ngā ratonga AT. Tirohia te rā o te wātaka me ngā āhuatanga ratonga me te urunga o nāianei i mua i te whakawhirinaki ki tētahi haerenga.

## Whakaritea, kātahi ka tirohia te taupānga kua tāutahia

1. Huakina a Along mā HTTPS i tētahi matapihi pūtirotiro noa, kaua i te tirotiro tūmataiti, i te pūtirotiro o tētahi taupānga pāpāho pāpori rānei. Huakina `https://reality2.ai/along/`.
2. Waiho kia tuwhera kia oti te tikiake tuatahi (tata ki te 40 MB, ā, me nui ake te mokowā i muri i te wetewete kōpeketanga). Huakina Ngā tautuhinga (Settings), ka tatari kia puta **kua rite mō te tuimotu** (offline ready).
3. Whāia ngā tohutohu tāuta mō tō pūnaha i raro nei.
4. Huakina **te ata Along kua tāutahia i a koe e tuihono tonu ana**. Tirohia anō Ngā tautuhinga, ka tatari kia oti ngā tikiake. Ka whakamahi ētahi pūnaha i tētahi rokiroki motuhake mō ngā taupānga kua tāutahia.
5. Whakawetohia te Wi-Fi me ngā raraunga pūkoro. Katia a Along, huakina anō mā tōna ata, ka whakamātau i tētahi rapunga wāhitau hou me tētahi haerenga. Tirohia hoki he mahere ara. Hono anō i muri iho.

Kāore te ata anake e whakaū kua rite mō te tuimotu. Ki te kore te whakamātau tuimotu e angitu, huakina te taupānga kua tāutahia i te tuihono, ka whakaoti i te whakarite i mua i te whakawhirinaki ki a ia.

## Kōwhiria tō pūtirotiro me tō pūnaha

| Pūnaha | Ngā ara tāuta kei tēnei aratohu |
| --- | --- |
| Windows | Chrome, Edge, Brave |
| Linux | Chrome, Edge, Brave; ka rerekē te whakaurunga ki te papamahi i runga i te tohatoha Linux |
| macOS | Chrome, Edge, Brave; Safari i te macOS Sonoma 14, i tētahi putanga hou ake rānei |
| Waea, papa rānei Android | Chrome; Edge, Brave rānei mēnā kei te wātea te mahi tāuta, mata kāinga rānei |
| iPhone/iPad | Safari; te mahi Share a Chrome; Share i Edge/Brave mēnā e wātea ana, ki te kore whakamahia Safari |
| Chromebook | Chrome |

Kāore a Safari i te kōwhiringa tāuta o nāianei mō Windows, Linux, Android rānei. Ka rerekē ngā ingoa tahua i runga i te putanga pūtirotiro. He tohutohu ēnei, ehara i te kī kua whakamātauria ā-tinana ngā huinga katoa. Whai muri i te tāuta, huakina i te tuihono, tirohia Ngā tautuhinga → kua rite mō te tuimotu, kātahi ka whakamātau i tētahi haerenga hou me te hononga kua whakawetohia.

## Chrome — Windows, Linux, macOS me Chromebook

1. Huakina a Along ki Chrome, ka whakaoti i te whakarite.
2. Kōwhiria te ata tāuta i te pae wāhitau, **⋮ → Cast, save and share → Install page as app** rānei. Whakaūngia te tāutanga.
3. Huakina a Along mā Start i Windows, te kaiwhakarewa taupānga i Linux, te kaiwhakarewa taupānga/Spotlight i macOS, te Launcher i Chromebook rānei. Kei `chrome://apps` hoki ngā taupānga kua tāutahia.
4. Whakamaua te taupānga ki tō paetaumahi, Dock, shelf rānei mā te tahua o tō pūnaha whakahaere.

[Ngā tohutohu papamahi a Google](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/9658361?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DDesktop).

## Edge — Windows, Linux me macOS

1. Huakina a Along ki Edge, ka whakaoti i te whakarite.
2. Kōwhiria te ata tāuta, **… → More tools → Apps → Install this site as an app** rānei. Kei te tahua matua tonu te **Apps** i ētahi putanga. Whakaūngia.
3. Huakina `edge://apps` hei kimi i a Along. I Windows, kei te whārangi Details ngā pokatata mō Start, te paetaumahi me te papamahi. I Linux/macOS, whakamahia te kaiwhakarewa taupānga o te pūnaha, ka whakamau i te taupānga e rere ana mēnā e hiahia ana koe; ka rerekē ngā pokatata e wātea ana.

[Ngā tohutohu tāuta me te whakahaere taupānga a Microsoft](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/edge/install-manage-or-uninstall-apps-in-microsoft-edge).

## Brave — Windows, Linux me macOS

1. Huakina a Along ki Brave, ka whakaoti i te whakarite.
2. Kōwhiria te ata tāuta mēnā ka kitea, whakamahia rānei **Save and Share → Install page as app** i te tahua matua. Ka whakamahi ētahi putanga i **Cast, save and share**. Whakaūngia.
3. Huakina a Along mā te kaiwhakarewa taupānga o tō pūnaha whakahaere, ka whakamau ki reira mēnā e hiahia ana koe. Puritia a Brave me te kōtaha i whakamahia mō te tāuta.

Ka whakamārama [te rōpū tautoko a Brave](https://community.brave.com/t/progressive-web-applications-pwa-in-brave/605917) i te tahua papamahi. Ko te pātene **Tāutahia a Along** (Install Along) i Ngā tautuhinga tētahi atu ara mēnā ka tukuna e te pūtirotiro. Ki te kore he mahi tāuta, whakamahia he pūtirotiro hou e tautokona ana; kāore te tohuwhārangi anake e whakaū i te whakarite tuimotu. Kāore anō kia oti te whakamātautau ā-tinana mō Brave.

## Safari — macOS

I te macOS Sonoma 14, i tētahi putanga hou ake rānei, huakina a Along ki Safari, ka kōwhiri **File → Add to Dock** (kei Share hoki). Whakaingoatia ko Along, kōwhiria Add, ka huaki mā Dock, Spotlight rānei. Tirohia te rite mō te tuimotu i roto i taua taupānga i te tuihono tonu; tērā pea he rokiroki motuhake tōna i tō Safari. I ngā putanga macOS tawhito ake, whakamahia he putanga Chrome, Edge, Brave rānei e tautokona tonutia ana, e taea ai te tāuta taupānga tukutuku. [Ngā tohutohu Mac a Apple](https://support.apple.com/en-ie/104996).

## Chrome — waea, papa rānei Android

Huakina a Along, whakaotia te whakarite, kātahi ka pā ki **⋮ → Install and create shortcut → Install**. I runga i te putanga, tērā pea ko **Install app**, ko **Add to Home screen** rānei te ingoa. Whakaūngia, ka huaki i te ata Along hou mā te mata kāinga, te kaiwhakarewa taupānga rānei. Tērā pea kei te wātea hoki Ngā tautuhinga → **Tāutahia a Along**. [Ngā tohutohu Android a Google](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/9658361?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DAndroid).

## Edge or Brave — waea, papa rānei Android

Huakina a Along, ka whakaoti i te whakarite. Whakamātauria tuatahitia **Tāutahia a Along** i Ngā tautuhinga mēnā ka kitea. Ki te kore, huakina te tahua pūtirotiro, ka kimi **Install app**, **Add to Home screen**, **Add to phone** rānei. Kōwhiria te mahi e wātea ana, ka whakaū i te whakanoho ata. Huakina taua ata i te tuihono, kātahi ka whakamātau anō i te tuimotu.

Ka whakawhirinaki te ingoa me te momo tāutanga — he taupānga motuhake, he pokatata mata kāinga rānei — ki te pūtirotiro me tōna putanga. Kāore anō kia whakamātauria ngā tahua pūkoro katoa. Ki te kore he mahi tāuta, huakina taua URL Along ki Chrome, ka whai i tōna ara kua tuhia; me tikiake anō mō taua pūtirotiro/kōtaha. Kaua e pōhēhē he rite te tāpiri makau ki te whakarite taupānga tuimotu. Ka whai [te kaupapa Android a Brave](https://github.com/brave/brave-browser/issues/25121) i te atanga tāuta mata kāinga; ka whakamārama [te āwhina pūkoro a Microsoft](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-edge/microsoft-edge-for-mobile-faqs-29296eab-b76f-4a87-ac9c-9835da53465d) i a Edge pūkoro, he rerekē ōna tahua i ō te papamahi.

## Safari — iPhone me iPad

Huakina a Along ki Safari, ka kōwhiri **Share → Add to Home Screen**. Whakamahia **View More**, **Edit Actions** rānei mēnā e hiahiatia ana. Whakakāngia **Open as Web App** mēnā ka tukuna, kātahi ka pā ki **Add**. Huakina te ata hou i te tuihono, ka tatari kia rite mō te tuimotu i roto i taua taupānga. Kāore anō kia oti te whakamātautau ā-tinana i a Along mō iOS/iPadOS. [Ngā tohutohu iPhone a Apple](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/open-as-web-app-iphea86e5236/ios), [ngā tohutohu iPad a Apple](https://support.apple.com/guide/ipad/open-as-web-app-ipad8f1f7a29/ipados).

## Chrome — iPhone me iPad

Huakina a Along ki Chrome. Whakamahia **Share → Add to Home Screen**, whakaūngia te ingoa, ka pā ki **Add**. Huakina te ata i te tuihono i mua i te whakamātau tuimotu. Ki te kore taua kōwhiringa, huakina te wāhitau ki Safari, ka whai i ngā tohutohu Safari. E ai ki a Google, tērā pea ka huakina he taupānga tukutuku, te pūtirotiro taunoa rānei e te ata mata kāinga, i runga i te mea e wātea ana: tirohia te hua, kaua e whakapae kua rawaka te ata. [Ngā tohutohu iPhone/iPad a Google](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/9658361?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DiOS).

## Edge or Brave — iPhone me iPad

Huakina te mahi Share o te pūtirotiro, ka whakamahi **Add to Home Screen** mēnā e wātea ana. Ki te kore, tāruatia te wāhitau Along ki Safari, ka whakamahi **Share → Add to Home Screen** ki reira. Kaua e whakamahi i ngā tohutohu papamahi pērā i **Install page as app** mō iOS. Kua tuhia motuhaketia e te rōpū tautoko Brave ngā herenga iOS i te tautoko papamahi; kāore tēnei aratohu e whakapae he ōrite ngā tahua o tētahi putanga pūkoro hou ake. Whakaotia te whakarite mā te ata kua tāutahia, ka whakamātau tuimotu. [Te whakautu tautoko a Brave](https://community.brave.com/t/progressive-web-applications-pwa-in-brave/605917), [te ara tāuta a Apple](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/open-as-web-app-iphea86e5236/ios).

## Ki te kore e taea te tāuta

Ka taea te whakamahi a Along i tētahi ripa pūtirotiro e tautokona ana, me te tohuwhārangi i tōna wāhitau HTTPS. Ka whakawhirinaki tonu te tuimotu ki te keteroki angitu o te taupānga me ngā raraunga i taua kōtaha pūtirotiro. Ehara te whārangi HTML kua tiakina, te whakaahua mata, te pokatata noa, te ZIP kua wetekina mā `file://` rānei i te tāutanga tuimotu. Ka rerekē ngā tahua; whāia ngā tohutohu mana mō tō pūtirotiro me tōna putanga. He whakamārama ara tāuta te rārangi pūnaha, ehara i te kī kua whakamātauria ā-tinana ngā pūnaha katoa.

## He aha ka mahi ki te kore te pae tukutuku?

| Ka wātea i muri i te whakarite | Me whai hononga |
| --- | --- |
| Rapu wāhitau me ngā tūnga, haerenga wātaka mā pahi/tereina/waka kōpiko/hīkoi | Te tikiake tuatahi me te whakaora i muri i te muku rokiroki pūtirotiro |
| Ngā haerenga kua tiakina, ngā whakaaro mō ō ritenga, ngā manakohanga | Ngā whakahou taupānga me te wātaka |
| Ngā ara AT, ngā tauwāhi tūnga me ngā wā tūnga kua tikiakehia | Ngā papamuri mahere tiriti tuihono kōwhiringa |
| Te tūhura ara/tūnga me te āwhina tāuta | Ngā matapae/pānui wā tūturu mēnā kua whirihorahia he tūmau; ngā hononga ki waho |

Ka whakamahi te taupānga kua tāutahia i te pūnaha whakahaere o tō pūtirotiro me tōna rokiroki ā-rohe. Puritia taua pūtirotiro. Ka taea e te muku raraunga pae, te huri pūtirotiro/kōtaha, te tango taupānga, te horoi rokiroki pūrere rānei te tango i ngā raraunga. Kei Ngā tautuhinga te rā paunga o te wātaka; kāore te tuimotu e tohu ka tika ngā wātaka mō ake tonu atu. Huakina i te tuihono i ētahi wā, ka whakamahi **Whakahoutia te wātaka kua tikiakehia** (Update downloaded timetable) me **Tirohia he whakahou taupānga** (Check for an app update).

## Ka noho ō raraunga ki konei

Ka tukatukahia ā-rohe ngā wāhitau ka patohia me ngā rapunga haerenga, ahakoa kei te tuihono. Ka penapenahia ā-rohe ngā ara me ngā ritenga kua akohia. Mā **Warewaretia taku hītori haerenga** (Forget my journey history) i Ngā tautuhinga e muku ngā haerenga kua akohia/kua tiakina; mā te whakaweto whakaaro e whakamutu te ako. Ka tango hoki te muku raraunga pae i ngā tikiake tuimotu. Whakamahia te raka mata o tō pūrere hei tiaki i ngā kōrero ā-rohe.

Ka tuku tonu ngā tikiake paetukutuku/raraunga noa i ngā kōrero hononga, pērā i tō wāhitau IP, ki te kaihautū. Ka tuku ngā mahere tiriti tuihono kōwhiringa i ngā tono mō te takiwā mahere e kitea ana ki OpenStreetMap. Ka whiwhi tono raraunga tētahi tūmau wā tūturu kua whirihorahia. Kāore a Along e tuku i ō wāhitau kua patohia, ō haerenga kua tiakina, tō hītori ritenga rānei me aua tono. Kāore a Along e whakahaere i ngā tārua haumaru pūrere/pūnaha whakahaere me ngā kaupapa here pūtirotiro.

He motuhake te rokiroki o ia pūtirotiro, kōtaha me te pūtakenga pae. Kāore te tāuta mai i tētahi URL tūmatanui hou e whakawhiti raraunga mai i tētahi pae tūmataiti/whakamātau. Kāore he pūkete kapua, he tārua haumaru kapua aunoa rānei mō ō haerenga i a Along.

## Urupare tūmatanui kōwhiringa

Kei Ngā tautuhinga, ngā taipitopito ara/tūnga me ngā tuhipoka haerenga/wehenga te
**Tukuna he urupare mō tēnei mata**. Ka puritia tō hukihuki ā-rohe, ahakoa tuimotu.
Arotakengia te tuhinga katoa i mua i te huaki i a GitHub. Me whai pūkete GitHub,
me kōwhiri hoki **Submit new issue** ki reira; he tūmatanui ngā pūrongo kua tukuna.
Kāore te huaki i a GitHub anake e whakaū kua tae atu. Whakapirihia te hononga take
ki Along hei tirotiro kua tae atu.

Kāore he wāhitau, he tauwāhi, he hītori haerenga rānei ka tāpirihia aunoatia. Ka
taea te kōwhiri kia whakaurua te putanga taupānga, te reo me te mata whānui e
whakaaturia ana. Kaua e pato kōrero whaiaro kāore koe e hiahia kia tūmatanui. Mā
**Mukua tēnei hukihuki** e tango te hukihuki ā-rohe; kāore e muku i tētahi take kua
tukuna kē ki GitHub.
